# Solar8 Design System v1.0

## Motto

Quality Today.
Peace of Mind Tomorrow.

---

# Design Philosophy

The Solar8 website should feel:

• Professional
• Calm
• Honest
• Premium
• Organised
• Easy to understand

Visitors should never feel overwhelmed.

Every page should reduce uncertainty and build trust.

---

# Design Principle

Clarity Builds Confidence.

Whenever a design decision is made ask:

Does this make Solar8 easier to understand?

If not,
don't add it.

---

# White Space

---

# Content Rules

Write for homeowners.

Avoid technical jargon.

Explain before selling.

Build trust before discussing products.

---

# Homepage Purpose

The homepage does NOT sell products.

The homepage sells trust.

The goal is one action:

Book a FREE Home Energy Consultation.

---

# Solar8 Standard

Every page must answer one question.

Every section must have one purpose.

Every button must have one action.

If something creates confusion,
remove it.

---

# Final Principle

Simple is harder.

Choose simple.
