# Solar8 Brand Guidelines

## Brand Name

Solar8

---

## Brand Promise

Helping South African families take control of their electricity costs.

---

## Vision

To become South Africa's most trusted online resource for reducing household electricity costs through education, practical tools, and quality solar solutions.

---

## Mission

We educate first, recommend second, and sell last.

Our goal is to empower homeowners and small businesses to make informed energy decisions with confidence.

---

## Brand Values

- Trust
- Simplicity
- Honesty
- Education
- Sustainability
- Long-term thinking

---

---

# Brand Personality

Solar8 is:

- Friendly
- Professional
- Helpful
- Knowledgeable
- Honest
- Optimistic

Solar8 is NOT:

- Pushy
- Overly technical
- Complicated
- Arrogant
- Sales-driven

---

# Tone of Voice

Write like an experienced advisor helping a neighbour.

Use plain English.

Explain technical concepts simply.

Focus on benefits before specifications.

Always educate before promoting products.

---

# Core Message

Solar8 helps South African families reduce monthly electricity costs through smarter energy decisions.
