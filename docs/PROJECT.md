# Solar8 Energy Platform

## Vision

Helping South African families take control of their electricity costs through trusted advice, practical tools, and quality solar solutions.

---

## Mission

Solar8 exists to educate, guide and support homeowners and small businesses in making informed energy decisions that reduce long-term electricity costs.

---

## Core Principles

- Education before sales
- Customer problems before products
- Simplicity over complexity
- Honest recommendations
- Long-term relationships
- Fast, modern user experience

---
